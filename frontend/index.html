Engineering Principle: Wi-Fi Signal vs. Internet Speed

Wi-Fi Signal (dBm) measures the physical RF link strength and attenuation between this laptop/phone and the Access Point antenna.
Internet Speed (Mbps) measures WAN data transfer throughput across your gateway router and ISP fiber uplink. A strong signal (-42 dBm) with low internet speed indicates a WAN/ISP bottleneck, not a wireless issue.
